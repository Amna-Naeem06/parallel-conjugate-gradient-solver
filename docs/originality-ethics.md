
# Originality and Ethics Declaration

We declare that this project, titled "Parallel Conjugate Gradient Solver", is being developed by our team as part of the CS426 Parallel and Distributed Computing course at Air University, Islamabad.

We commit to the following principles:

1. All implementations will be developed and understood by the team members.
2. External libraries, tools, references, and resources will be properly acknowledged.
3. No experimental results, performance measurements, or benchmark data will be fabricated or misrepresented.
4. All performance comparisons will be conducted under documented and reproducible conditions.
5. Code obtained or adapted from external sources will be appropriately attributed.
6. Every team member will contribute to the project and understand its overall implementation.
7. We will maintain academic integrity and follow the university's academic guidelines.
